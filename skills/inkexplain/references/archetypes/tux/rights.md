## Rights and restrictions

- Tux: Larry Ewing allows use and modification of the image provided he and The GIMP are acknowledged if someone asks. Credit line: "Tux by Larry Ewing, created with The GIMP."
- The word "Linux": use it factually and as an adjective (for example, "Linux kernel"), keep the ownership attribution to Linus Torvalds in credits, and never use it in a way that implies endorsement.
- Restrictions for every image, label, caption, and report:
  - No distro logos and no Linux Foundation logos.
  - No claims of endorsement, certification, compatibility, affiliation, sponsorship, or approval.
  - No product advertising, product posters, CLI screenshots, web hero graphics, kernel dashboards, or operating-system marketing graphics.
- The rights note in `prompts.md` carries the credit and restrictions into every prompt and delivery report.
