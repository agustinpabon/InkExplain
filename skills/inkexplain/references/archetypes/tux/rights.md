# Tux Rights

## Source

- Archetype: Tux (`tux`), category `mascot`, status `active`.
- Character: Tux, the Linux penguin, created by Larry Ewing using The GIMP.
- Source: Larry Ewing's Linux 2.0 Penguins page, isc.tamu.edu/~lewing/linux/. It is no longer online (HTTP 404 on 2026-10-16, for the whole host). An archived copy is at https://web.archive.org/web/20260409031334/https://isc.tamu.edu/~lewing/linux/, and the Linux kernel carries the same terms in `Documentation/images/COPYING-logo` (https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/Documentation/images/COPYING-logo).
- Rights holder: Larry Ewing.
- License: free to use and modify. His page grants permission "provided you acknowledge me ... and The GIMP if someone asks"; the kernel's notice says Tux "is freely usable as long as you acknowledge Larry as the original artist". This pack always credits both Larry Ewing and The GIMP. A vector redraw by Garrett LeSage and IFo Hancroft is usable on the same terms, crediting all three; this pack bundles neither version.
- Word mark: Linux is the registered trademark of Linus Torvalds in the U.S. and other countries.
- Trademark guidance: Linux Foundation trademark usage, https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/trademark-usage, and the Linux mark, https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/the-linux-mark.
- Tux image attribution and Linux word-mark guidance are separate concerns: Larry Ewing's terms cover the penguin; the trademark guidance covers the word "Linux".
- Bundled files: none. The identity markers in `identity.md` were transcribed from a reference image that is not bundled; the marker list is the identity authority. For provenance, that image was a 3500 x 2300 JPEG with SHA-256 `071bc327e4a2814864f9e2fcdea99aa50482a92ef4e816de9d9ce5f40e17bb2a`.

## Rights and restrictions

- Tux: Larry Ewing allows use and modification of the image provided he is acknowledged as the original artist, and The GIMP when asked. Credit both whenever Tux images are published. Credit line: "Tux by Larry Ewing, created with The GIMP."
- The word "Linux": use it factually and as an adjective (for example, "Linux kernel"), keep the ownership attribution to Linus Torvalds in credits, and never use it in a way that implies endorsement.
- Restrictions for every image, label, caption, and report:
  - No distro logos and no Linux Foundation logos.
  - No claims of endorsement, certification, compatibility, affiliation, sponsorship, or approval.
  - No product advertising, product posters, CLI screenshots, web hero graphics, kernel dashboards, or operating-system marketing graphics.
- The rights note in `prompts.md` carries the credit and restrictions into every prompt and delivery report.
