# Littlebox Identity

Littlebox is a recurring closed paper-box character: a small, calm, deadpan operator that turns ideas, evidence, tasks, and systems into structure. It appears in every image of this archetype.

## Identity

- **State**: always closed (`Littlebox state: closed`).
- **View**: front-left three-quarter. The front panel is the largest face; one narrower side panel and the closed top lid are visible.
- **Body**: compact and squat, the front panel slightly wider than tall; white or near-white, never tan, kraft brown, beige, or realistic cardboard. Fat, rough, wobbly black marker outline; on the default white page that outline alone carries the shape.
- **Face**: two small dot eyes centered on the upper-middle front panel. No mouth, smile, eyebrows, blush, costume, or accessories.
- **Legs**: tiny, short vertical black strokes under the front panel.
- **Arms**: side-seam twig arms whenever Littlebox handles, carries, pushes, catches, sorts, points, or holds: thin, curved, branch-like black strokes without hands, gloves, fingers, or sleeves. At most two, one from each side seam; never from the face, lid, lower body, eyes, or a prop.
- **Lid**: two top flaps meet along one central seam that runs front-to-back across the top, perpendicular to the front edge.
- **Tape**: exactly one amber seam tape on that seam, narrow (about 10%-15% of the top width), black-outlined when large enough. Its short front tab hangs over the top front edge as a jagged torn front tab with two or three sawtooth teeth, facing the viewer. Never a side-wall sticker, panel patch, loose prop, arrow, or extra tape marks.
- It reads as a compact paper box, never a laptop, treasure chest, toolbox, pizza box, bucket, tray, shopping bag, crate, or mailbox.

## Series lock

- **Locked** in a series: the closed state, the front-left three-quarter view, the compact squat body with its rough marker outline, two dot eyes on the upper-middle front panel, tiny legs, at most two side-seam twig arms, two top flaps meeting at one seam, exactly one amber seam tape with its jagged front tab, and one flat page color.
- **Free**: position, scale (it fills about 35%-55% of the canvas), what it carries, and the acting of its twig arms and tiny legs.
- **Rejected**: an open or half-open box, a missing or second tape, a mouth, hands, or extra arms, a different body color, and a page color that changes between images.

## Recognition

Markers: box body, dot eyes, front-left three-quarter view, closed top flaps, tiny legs, side-seam twig arms when acting, and the single amber seam tape with its jagged front tab.

- Keep at least four across transformations, and at least five in final article images.
- Whenever they would be visible, the dot eyes, compact body, closed lid, twig arms (when acting), and jagged front tab are required.

## Failure modes

- Littlebox is decoration: removing it leaves the metaphor intact.
- Open, half-open, cutaway, or raised flaps, any visible interior, or a composition that depends on opening the box.
- Missing twig arms in an action scene; extra or lower arms, tail-like helper lines, a wrong arm origin, mascot hands, fingers, gloves, or sleeves.
- Tape missing, misplaced, duplicated, or so wide it reads as an orange flap; a smooth rectangular front tab instead of the jagged one; amber patches on panels or props.
- Eyes off the front panel, or the three-quarter face lost.
- A tan, brown, beige, or cardboard body; a tinted or shaded body fill or a drop shadow added to separate the box from a white page; a box that reads as another object or stretches into a carton, tray, bridge, machine, pipe, or anonymous package.
- Thin, smooth, ruler-straight, auto-traced, thresholded, posterized, or clean vector linework.
- A smiling cute mascot, accessories, or a delivery-company icon look.
