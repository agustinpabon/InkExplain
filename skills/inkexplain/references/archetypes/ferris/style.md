# Ferris Style

Sparse article illustrations in which Ferris performs one action inside an original, Rust-themed article metaphor. Ferris stays recognizable inside the simplified drawing; recognition matters more than detail.

## Canvas

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration by default.
- Clean white or near-white page with generous whitespace.
- One core idea and one enlarged action subject. Ferris's main action takes about 35–55% of the canvas, with empty space around it so the metaphor reads at a glance.

## Linework

- Rough hand-drawn black linework with visible human looseness.
- Ferris is drawn in the same loose line as everything else: simplified, never a detailed mascot render.
- No polished vector finish.

## Color

- Black: main strokes, objects, Ferris's details, and labels.
- Orange or rust-red: Ferris and one selected action emphasis.
- Red: only warning flags, motion marks, stamps, or decisive emphasis.
- No gradients, shadows, or paper texture.

## Labels

- Usually two to six concise labels, copied exactly in the user's language. For non-Latin scripts, preserve the script and never translate.
- Rough handwriting, never clean digital font labels.
- Nouns or short verb phrases, placed near objects, paths, gates, warnings, review points, or outputs.
- Keep labels away from Ferris's eyes, claws, legs, and shell silhouette.
- When labels start to crowd the canvas, move the explanation into the article text.

## Avoid

- Dense explanatory text, formal PPT diagrams, dense course slides, posters, and crowded nodes, arrows, labels, or panels.
- Official Rust diagram layouts, code screenshots, and IDE, terminal, or UI scenes.
- Rust or Cargo logos and logo-forward compositions.
- A beach crab, seafood icon, random red animal, or generic crab mascot in place of Ferris.
