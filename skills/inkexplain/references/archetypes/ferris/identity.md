# Ferris Identity

Ferris the Rustacean, adapted for sparse article illustrations. Ferris appears in every image of this archetype as the active operator of the article's idea.

## Identity

Keep these cues together:

- a compact orange or rust-red crab body;
- a broad crab shell silhouette;
- two visible claws;
- small legs;
- two eyes or eyestalk eyes;
- active body language tied to the article idea.

Keep Ferris simple enough for article explanation and recognizable enough that no one mistakes it for a generic crab.

## Recognition

- Several cues read together in every image: body color, shell silhouette, claws, legs, and eyes; one cue alone is not Ferris.
- Ferris's claws, eyes, shell angle, legs, and body direction point toward the action.
- Ferris keeps the same design across a series; vary the object and the action before touching identity cues.
- Ferris sits inside the action, never beside it as a spectator.

## Failure modes

- **Generic-crab drift**: a generic crab, beach crab, seafood icon, random red animal, or vague crab silhouette.
- **Passive Ferris placement**: Ferris is tiny, decorative, removable, or only reacting, or stands beside the concept while props, arrows, or labels carry the meaning.
- **Over-detailed mascot rendering**: a character-sheet study instead of a sparse article sketch.
- **Source-asset tracing**: Ferris traced, recreated, or pose-matched from existing artwork, public mascot asset sheets, or official logo arrangements.
- **Rust-logo misuse**: Rust or Cargo logos, bundled Rust marks, official project marks as the main visual carrier, or a logo-forward layout around Ferris.
- **Official-affiliation cues**: badges, marks, wording, or composition that present Ferris as an official Rust project endorsement, approval, affiliation, or release status.

## Participation test

If removing Ferris leaves the metaphor intact, Ferris is decoration. Rewrite the prompt so Ferris performs the action.
