# Ferris Rights

## Source

- Archetype: Ferris (`ferris`), category `mascot`, status `active`.
- Character: Ferris the Rustacean, the crab commonly used as the Rust community mascot.
- Creator: Karen Rustad Tolva. Rust Foundation materials describe her as the original graphic designer of Ferris.
- Source site: rustacean.net, which presents a CC0 public-domain dedication (copyright waiver) for Ferris the Rustacean that names Karen Rustad Tolva.
- License: CC0 public-domain dedication.
- Bundled files: none. This pack ships no Ferris artwork; the cues in `identity.md` are written descriptions.

## Rights and restrictions

- Credit line, kept with published images and derived documentation: "Ferris the Rustacean was created by Karen Rustad Tolva and dedicated to the public domain (CC0) via rustacean.net."
- Separately from the dedication, Rust, Cargo, the Rust project's marks and logos, official-affiliation claims, and endorsement wording are governed by the Rust Foundation trademark policy.
- Restrictions for every image, label, prompt, and report:
  - No Rust or Cargo logos.
  - No claims of official Rust project affiliation or endorsement.
- Rust-themed props remain allowed as plain, logo-free article metaphors (see `motion-and-acting.md`).
- Maintainers verify the current wording on rustacean.net and in the Rust Foundation trademark policy before changing this archetype's status, publishing new samples, or broadening distribution claims.
