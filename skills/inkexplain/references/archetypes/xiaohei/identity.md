# Xiaohei Identity

Xiaohei is a small, serious, slightly absurd worker: a solid-black creature that runs part of the system the image explains. It is not decoration or a cute sticker. It appears in every image of this archetype and performs the central action.

## Identity

- **Body**: a solid black body, filled flat with black ink, with a slightly irregular hand-drawn outline. The silhouette may adapt to the job (a cylinder, a bean, a small block, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, a dark lump inside a machine) as long as it stays one compact solid-black shape.
- **Eyes**: two white dot eyes, small and round, set in the upper part of the body. No pupils, highlights, sparkles, or eyelashes.
- **Legs**: thin legs, drawn as single black strokes. Thin arms appear only when the action needs hands, and are drawn the same way.
- **Face**: a deadpan expression: blank, calm, a little dazed, and completely serious. No mouth unless a tiny line is needed for the action; no smile, blush, eyebrows, or tears.
- **Extras**: none. No costume, hat, accessory, logo, or text on the body.

## Recognition

Markers: solid black body, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan expression, and the irregular hand-drawn outline.

- All four registry markers (solid black body, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan expression) are required in every image.
- Xiaohei stays the same size class and drawing weight across a set, even when the body shape adapts to the job.
- Removing Xiaohei must break the metaphor. If the idea still reads without it, Xiaohei is decoration; rewrite the action so Xiaohei performs it.

## Failure modes

- Cute drift: a rounded, glossy, big-eyed mascot, sparkling eyes, a smile, blush, a costume, or a sticker or emoji look.
- Children's-cartoon or commercial-illustration rendering, or a body so polished it reads as a brand character.
- A grey, outlined-only, gradient-filled, or shaded body instead of flat solid black.
- Pupils, highlights, or coloured eyes instead of plain white dot eyes.
- Thick limbs, hands with fingers, gloves, or shoes instead of thin single-stroke legs and arms.
- Passive placement: Xiaohei stands in a corner watching while arrows, props, or labels carry the idea.
- Xiaohei crowds the structure: so large or detailed that the idea it explains is lost.
