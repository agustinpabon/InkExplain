# Xiaohei Identity

Xiaohei is a small, serious, slightly absurd worker: a solid-black figure that runs part of the system the image explains. It is not decoration or a cute sticker. It appears in every image of this archetype and has a part in the idea: it acts on the object, or guides, observes, or accompanies the flow.

`references/archetypes/xiaohei/xiaohei.png` is the bundled identity reference: a crop of one of its creator's own drawings (the source and the license are in `rights.md`). It defines who Xiaohei is, never a pose, a prop, or a layout. It also shows things that are not identity: a faint ink texture in the lower body (the fill is flat black), the sign and its pole, and a short stroke at its lower left, which is the second arm hanging at rest.

## Identity

- **Body**: one compact solid black body, a smooth rounded dome or bean: a rounded top, a flat or gently rounded bottom, about 1.2 to 1.5 times as tall as wide, filled with flat, uniform black ink and a slightly irregular hand-drawn outline. Nothing sticks out of the top or the sides except the limbs: no ears, horns, tufts, bumps, or corners.
- **Eyes**: two small round white dot eyes, side by side in the upper third of the body, each about one eighth as wide as the body. Plain white: no pupils, highlights, sparkles, eyelashes, or eyebrows. They are the only white on the body: no white line, outline, or mark.
- **Legs**: exactly two thin legs, each one single black stroke from the bottom edge with a tiny foot tick, about a third as long as the body is tall.
- **Arms**: none unless the action needs hands. At most two, each one thin single black stroke that leaves the body at its side outline and never crosses the front of the black fill (an arm drawn across it needs a white outline, which breaks the flat black), ending in a plain stroke tip: no fingers, gloves, mittens, or hollow outlined tubes.
- **Face**: a deadpan expression: blank, calm, a little dazed, and completely serious. No mouth, nose, smile, blush, eyebrows, or tears.
- **Extras**: none. No costume, hat, accessory, logo, or text on the body. Not a cat or any other animal: no ears, tail, snout, whiskers, or fur.

## Series lock

Within a series the identity does not move while the action does.

- **Free** (may change from image to image): where Xiaohei stands in the frame; its size, between about a sixth and a third of the frame's height; which way it faces; its acting (leaning, bracing, reaching, carrying, crouching); the angle of its arms and legs; and what it holds, pulls, pushes, or stamps.
- **Locked** (rejected when any of it changes): the body's shape class, a smooth dome or bean; two plain white dot eyes, about one eighth of the body wide, with no pupils, and no other white on the body; exactly two thin single-stroke legs, about a third as long as the body is tall; at most two thin single-stroke arms; no mouth, ears, tail, or snout; the flat black fill; the thin hand-drawn ink style and its weight.
- **Never allowed to change**: the species (a plain black figure, never a cat, a bunny, a ghost, a robot, or a person), the facial anatomy, the number of limbs, the ears (there are none), the tail (there is none), the body's identity, the eye design, and the drawing style. Xiaohei never becomes the prop: if the metaphor needs a funnel, a hole, or a box, draw that object and let Xiaohei work it.

## Recognition

Markers: solid black body, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan expression, and the irregular hand-drawn outline.

- All four registry markers (solid black body, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan expression) are required in every image.
- Xiaohei keeps the same body shape, eye design, limb weight, and size class across a set. Only its pose and what it handles change.
- Xiaohei has a part in the idea. If the picture reads the same with Xiaohei moved to another scene, Xiaohei is decoration: give it a part at the point of the idea (it acts on the object, or guides, observes, or accompanies the flow).

## Failure modes

- Cat drift: ears, horns, tufts, or bumps on the head, a tail, a snout or whiskers, or animal eyes. Two arms raised on either side of the head also read as ears, so keep the arms low and out to the side unless the action lifts something overhead.
- Shape drift: the body becomes a funnel, a hole, a shadow, a box, or a blob, or is a different shape from one image to the next.
- Limb drift: thick, tapered, doubled, fused, hollow, or extra arms or legs; hands with fingers, gloves, mittens, or shoes instead of thin single-stroke legs and arms.
- Face drift: pupils, highlights, or colored or sparkling eyes instead of plain white dot eyes; a mouth, nose, or eyebrows.
- Texture: hatching, scribbles, fur, grey, or a gradient inside the body, including copying faint ink marks from the reference. The fill is one flat black.
- White on the body: a white line, outline, or halo where an arm crosses the black fill. Draw the arm from the side outline instead, so the only white on the body is the two eyes.
- Split body: a belt, bar, rail, pipe, or desk edge drawn across the front of the body, so that the black fill reads as two shapes. Draw each object beside Xiaohei or behind it.
- Proportion drift: pinpoint eyes, stubby legs, or a body that shrinks to a small figure in a big scene, against the reference's eyes about one eighth of the body wide and legs about a third as long as the body is tall.
- Cute drift: a rounded, glossy, big-eyed mascot, a smile, blush, a costume, or a sticker or emoji look.
- Children's-cartoon or commercial-illustration rendering, or a body so polished it reads as a brand character.
- Passive placement: Xiaohei stands in a corner, looking at nothing the picture explains, while arrows, props, or labels carry the idea. A Xiaohei beside the step that matters, turned toward it or pointing at it, is guiding, not passive.
- Xiaohei crowds the structure: so large or detailed that the idea it explains is lost.
